01 / The story

Not the degree on the certificate

I chose chemistry and I am staying in it. Almost everything I build sits next to it rather than inside it, and this page is the long answer to why.

IIT Kharagpur · 2024–28

I came to IIT Kharagpur through the Jawahar Navodaya Vidyalaya system and the Dakshana scholarship, which is a long way of saying I did not grow up around computers. I picked up programming here, mostly by building things I wanted to exist: a weather app because the campus forecast was useless, a billing system because a shop near home was still using a notebook.

The data side came later, through coursework in statistical inference and then a lot of SQL on my own. The product side came from working with a small jewellery brand, where I learned that a good dashboard is worth nothing if nobody changes what they do because of it.

I am in my chemistry degree by choice and I like it, but I have been honest with myself that the work I reach for first is technical. So I have kept three resumes rather than pretending to have settled the question early.

Outside of that: I play kho-kho and badminton, swim when the pool is open, sing badly but often, and I mentor JEE aspirants through the Dakshana alumni network.
